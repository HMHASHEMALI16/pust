Community guidelines

What reviews are for

At PUST, reviews are to hold teachers accountable and give the next batch an idea what to expect. Focus on teaching, grading, workload, difficulty, attendance.

  • Focus on course and teaching, not the person.
  • Be specific. “Explains with examples” beats “great teacher.”
  • Be fair. One bad exam is one experience.
  • Don’t include details that identify you.

What we remove

  • Profanity, slurs, discriminatory language.
  • Accusations of criminal behaviour (report via university process, not anon claims).
  • Personal attacks on appearance, religion, family, sexuality, disability.
  • Anything identifying another student.
  • Spam, ads, off-topic rants, fake course reviews.

What we're not responsible for

Reviews are anonymous student opinions. We provide the space; we don’t write or endorse them. One experience isn’t the final word. Professors may email for a claim review.

Your anonymity, plainly

The reviews store has no author column. A separate duplicate-check notes “you reviewed this course” apart from content. Sign-in with OTP to your student email @s.pust.ac.bd only proves you’re a PUST student (teacher/admin @pust.ac.bd emails are blocked). Moderators never see authors.
